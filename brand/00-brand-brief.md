# Brand Brief — LaunchSite PH *(working name)*

## The one-line positioning

We build your complete professional online presence in 7 days — powered by AI, crafted by a human.

## What we actually sell

Not websites. **Credibility.** A Filipino professional with only a Facebook page looks
informal. A clean, fast, well-written website makes them look established. That is the
product. The website is the delivery mechanism.

## Who we're for

Licensed and credentialed professionals, and owner-operated businesses, who already have
customers and now need to look the part. They have money, they're time-poor, and they are
allergic to jargon.

Tier 1 (best fit): clinics, dental practices, law firms, architects, accounting firms,
real estate brokers, financial advisors, consultants, resorts, restaurants.

Tier 2: gyms, salons, beauty and derma clinics, veterinary clinics, schools, churches,
contractors, coaches, speakers, photographers.

## Why they choose us over an agency

| Traditional agency | Us |
|---|---|
| 30-90 days | 7-14 days from content handoff |
| PHP 40,000 and up | From PHP 10,000 |
| Multiple meetings | One questionnaire, one call |
| Slow revision cycles | AI-assisted revisions in hours |
| Quote on request | Prices published openly |

Never attack agencies by name or imply they are incompetent. Just show the difference.

## Brand personality

Premium, minimal, confident, quiet. Think engineering firm, not marketing hype.

- **We are:** precise, calm, transparent, fast, plain-spoken
- **We are not:** loud, salesy, emoji-heavy, discount-driven, "growth hacker"

## Voice rules

- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No superlatives we cannot prove.
- Numbers over adjectives: "7 days," "3 revision rounds," not "lightning fast."
- Never say "we use ChatGPT." Say: *"We combine current AI tooling with human design
  judgement to cut cost and turnaround without cutting quality."*
- Answer the unspoken question early: *"Is this just AI?"* -> **AI builds faster. A human
  builds it right.**

## Things we never say

- "Guaranteed results" / "rank #1 on Google" / "more patients" / "more clients"
- "Cheapest in the Philippines" — we compete on speed and clarity, not price
- Anything implying a team of employees exists when it does not. Solo is fine.
  Present systems and process, not fictional staff.

## The trust problem to solve on-site

A visitor's real fear is: *"Will this person disappear after I pay?"*
Counter it with: a real photo and name, published prices, a written process with dated
stages, a public revision policy, and a project tracking link they get after signing.
