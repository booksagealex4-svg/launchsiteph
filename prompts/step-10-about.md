# Step 10 — About Page

Build the /about page for LaunchSite PH. Existing tokens, header, footer and <Reveal>. Do not modify other pages.

The job of this page is to remove the fear "will this person disappear after I pay?" Be personal and concrete, not corporate.

1. HERO ROW — two columns, stacked on mobile:
   - Left: H1 "You are hiring a person, not an agency." Body: "[Your name]. [X] years in publishing and creative production, including work with international clients. I now build websites for Philippine professionals using current AI tooling, which is how a site that would cost PHP 40,000 elsewhere starts at PHP 10,000 here."
   - Right: a portrait photo placeholder, radius 14px, 1px border, with a soft primary glow behind it. Not a logo — a real photo goes here.

2. "WHY SOLO IS A FEATURE" panel: bordered, max-w-3xl. "No account manager. No handoffs between departments. You talk to the person building your site, from the first message to launch day."

3. VALUES — three cards:
   - Transparent by default — "Published prices, written scope, fixed delivery dates. Nothing is negotiated in the dark."
   - Fast, without being careless — "AI removes the repetitive work, not the review. Nothing ships that a person has not checked."
   - Built to be handed over — "You own your domain, your content and your site. No lock-in."

4. TIMELINE — a vertical timeline of career milestones, each with year, title, one line. Use placeholder entries the user will edit, clearly marked with [brackets].

5. TOOLS STRIP: a wrapped row of small bordered chips on surface — React, Next.js, Supabase, Vercel, Figma, Claude, GitHub, Google Analytics. Heading: "The tools behind the work." Muted note below: "The tools change. The standard does not."

6. CREDENTIALS ROW: placeholder slots for business registration, certifications and client logos, each a bordered box with [placeholder] labels the user replaces later.

7. CLOSING CTA: "Tell me what you do. I will tell you what it costs." with a primary button to /contact.

MOTION: standard reveal with stagger. Nothing elaborate on this page — restraint reads as confidence.

Do not change: header, footer, other pages, tokens.
