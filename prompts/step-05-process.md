# Step 5 — The 7-Day Process

Add a process timeline section below the benefits grid on the LaunchSite PH home page. Existing tokens and <Reveal> only. Do not modify sections already built.

Use the light band treatment here: light-bg background with light-text, to break up the dark page. Adjust card and border colors accordingly (white cards, subtle grey borders, primary stays the same).

Section header:
- Eyebrow: "How it works"
- H2: "From first message to live website in seven days."

Timeline: horizontal on desktop (7 nodes connected by a line), vertical on mobile (line down the left with nodes beside it). Each node has a day chip, a title, and one line of body:

1. Day 0 - Inquiry - "You send a message or book a free 20-minute call."
2. Day 1 - Quote and scope - "You get a fixed written quote and a package recommendation."
3. Day 1-2 - Questionnaire - "A short form covering your services, credentials, hours and photos."
4. Day 2 - Content handoff - "You send logo, photos and text. The delivery clock starts here."
5. Day 3-5 - Draft build - "First working version, live on a preview link."
6. Day 5-7 - Revisions - "Your changes applied, 2 to 5 rounds depending on package."
7. Day 7 - Launch - "Domain connected, site live, tracking installed, walkthrough call."

Highlight node 4 visually (filled primary dot, slightly larger) since it is the milestone that starts the clock.

Below the timeline, a small note in muted text, centered:
"Timelines assume content arrives on schedule. If your photos take two weeks, the build takes two weeks. We will tell you exactly what we need up front."

MOTION: as the section enters the viewport, the connecting line draws in left-to-right (desktop) or top-to-bottom (mobile) over 800ms, with nodes fading in at 100ms intervals as the line reaches them. Under prefers-reduced-motion, render fully drawn with no animation.

Do not change: any earlier section, header, footer, tokens.
