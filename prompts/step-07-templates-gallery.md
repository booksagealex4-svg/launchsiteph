# Step 7 — Templates Gallery Page

Build the /templates page for LaunchSite PH. Existing tokens, header, footer and <Reveal>. Do not modify the home page.

PAGE HEADER:
- H1: "Templates built around how your industry sells."
- Sub: "Eight flagship designs, each adapted to your branding, content and services. Do not see your industry? We adapt the closest one."

FILTER BAR: pill buttons that filter the grid client-side — All, Healthcare, Professional Services, Hospitality, Personal Brand. Active pill in primary. Filtering is instant, no page reload, with a 200ms fade on the grid.

1. Meridian - Healthcare - "Clinical and calm. Credentials, services and clinic hours, front and centre."

3. Verdict - Professional Services - "Authoritative and restrained. Practice areas, attorney profiles, consultation form."
4. Ledger - Professional Services - "Clear and credible. Services, qualifications and a consultation request."
5. Plateau - Professional Services - "Minimal, image-led. Your projects do the talking."
6. Ember - Hospitality - "Warm and appetite-driven. Menu, photography, reservations."
7. Tidewater - Hospitality - "Calm and spacious. Rooms, amenities, booking inquiry."
8. Anthem - Personal Brand - "Bold typography and video. Built for coaches, speakers and consultants."

BELOW THE GRID — "Available on request" section: a simple wrapped list of pill tags on surface background, with the note "We adapt the closest flagship template to your industry, at no extra cost." Tags: Real Estate, Insurance, Veterinary, Beauty and Derma Clinics, Salons, Gyms and Fitness, Schools, Churches, Construction, Car Dealerships, Photography, Medical Laboratories, Consultants, Event Services, Travel Agencies.

CLOSING CTA: bordered panel — "Not sure which fits? Tell us what you do and we will recommend one." with a primary button to /contact.

MOTION: cards reveal in a 60ms stagger on load and on filter change.

Do not change: header, footer, home page, tokens.
