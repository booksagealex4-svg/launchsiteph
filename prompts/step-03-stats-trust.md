# Step 3 — Stats and Trust Strip

Add a stats band directly below the hero on the LaunchSite PH home page. Use existing tokens and <Reveal>. Do not touch the hero, header, or footer.

Layout: a full-width band on `surface` background with a 1px top and bottom border. Four stat blocks in a row on desktop, 2x2 grid on mobile.

Stats (number in Sora 700 at h2 scale in primary, label below in text-muted, small):
- "7 Days" / "Typical delivery from content handoff"
- "8" / "Industry templates ready to adapt"
- "100%" / "Mobile-optimised, every build"
- "1 Year" / "Domain and hosting included"

ANIMATION: numeric values count up from 0 when the band enters the viewport, over 1.2 seconds, once only. "7 Days" counts the 7, "1 Year" counts the 1, "100%" counts to 100. Under prefers-reduced-motion, show final values immediately with no counting.

Below the stats, inside the same band, add a single centered reassurance line in text-muted:
"Fixed quotes. Written timelines. You talk to the person building your site."

RESPONSIVE: 2x2 on mobile with generous vertical gaps; numbers must not wrap.

Do not change: hero, header, footer, tokens.
