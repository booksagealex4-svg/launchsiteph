# Philippine Market Notes

Practical realities this site must be designed around. Verify anything time-sensitive
before publishing it as a claim on the site.

## Devices and connection

Philippine web traffic skews heavily to mobile, often mid-range Android on mobile data.
Consequences for the build:
- Mobile layout is the real layout. Desktop is the adaptation.
- Keep JavaScript minimal. No animation libraries. Lazy-load every image below the fold.
- Avoid autoplaying video in the hero. Use a lightweight image or CSS mockup instead.
- Tap targets minimum 48px. Thumb-reachable primary CTA on mobile.

## How people actually contact businesses here

Facebook Messenger and Viber outrank email for first contact, and mobile numbers outrank
landlines. The contact section must include:
- Messenger link
- Mobile number as a `tel:` link
- Email
- A form, for people who prefer not to identify themselves yet

Do not make email the only option.

## Facebook is the incumbent, not the enemy

Most target clients already have a Facebook Page and it works for them. Positioning the
website as a *replacement* creates resistance. Position it as the thing Facebook cannot do:
be found on Google, hold credentials and pricing, look permanent, and be linked in a
professional bio.

## Payment expectations

For projects in the PHP 10,000-20,000 range, buyers here generally want to talk to a person
before paying. That is why this site is inquiry-driven with no checkout. Payment is arranged
directly — commonly GCash, Maya, or bank transfer, often as a downpayment plus balance on
launch. State your terms in the quote, not on the website.

## Professional advertising restrictions

Physicians, dentists and lawyers in the Philippines operate under professional ethics rules
that restrict advertising and solicitation (PRC-administered codes of ethics; for lawyers,
the Code of Professional Responsibility and Accountability). Copy on templates and landing
pages aimed at these professions must stay informational and credibility-focused.
Confirm the current rules before publishing vertical-specific pages — these codes have been
revised in recent years.

## Trust signals worth adding when available

- Business registration (DTI or SEC) shown in the footer
- Ability to issue an official receipt
- A real photo and full name, not a logo alone
- A physical city, even without a full street address
- Named client testimonials with business names, once you have them

## Pricing context

Agency and studio quotes for small business sites in Metro Manila commonly start well above
PHP 40,000, while low-end freelance work clusters far below. Publishing fixed prices in the
PHP 10,000-20,000 band positions you clearly between the two. Verify current market rates
periodically rather than treating any single figure as fixed.
