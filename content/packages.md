- Launch -> Momentum buys **pages and gallery**: more surface area, more build hours.
- Momentum -> Authority buys **copywriting, blog, and real SEO**: the parts that take
  actual thinking time rather than assembly time.
- Care Plan is the recurring revenue. Pitch it at handover, when trust is highest.
