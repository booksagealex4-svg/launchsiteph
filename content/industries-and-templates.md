# Industries and Templates

## The rule

Build **8 real template demos**. List the rest as "available on request."
Eight excellent demos beat thirty half-finished ones, and every demo you publish is one
you must maintain.

studios, schools, churches, construction and contracting, car dealerships, photography,

Label them: *"Available on request — we adapt the closest flagship template to your industry."*

## Vertical-specific copy warning

For medical, dental and legal templates, the marketing copy must stay on credibility and
information, never patient or client acquisition. Philippine professional bodies restrict
advertising and solicitation for these professions. Use language like *"present your
credentials clearly"* and *"make your clinic easy to find and contact"* — never
*"get more patients."* Verify current PRC and IBP/CPRA rules before publishing.
